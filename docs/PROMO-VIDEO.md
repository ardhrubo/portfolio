# Portfolio promo video

The finished exports are `public/media/portfolio-promo-vertical.mp4` (1080×1920, 9:16) and `public/media/portfolio-promo-widescreen.mp4` (1920×1080, 16:9). Both are 24 seconds, 30 fps, H.264 with yuv420p, stereo AAC at 48 kHz, and fast-start MP4 metadata. They are local deliverables; nothing has been posted to social accounts.

The seven scenes introduce the portfolio and its maker, showcase Brown’s Interior, Squicy UAP, and ScrapeAutomate, then close with ardhrubo.dev and the public contact email. Text is embedded for silent viewing. The vertical layout is composed separately with generous top/bottom margins. Preview boards and posters are included. English VTT captions and the website transcript provide a text alternative. There is no spoken narration.

The soundtrack is an original, deterministic synthesis of chords, melody, percussion, and transition accents, generated by the renderer; it contains no sampled commercial song. The source WAV is included. Existing project screenshots and portrait are used, and fonts retain their licenses under `public/fonts/`.

## Rebuild

Requires Python 3, Pillow with WOFF2 font support, numpy, Node.js, and FFmpeg with libx264/AAC. No additional website runtime dependencies are needed.

```sh
python3 scripts/render-promo.py --preview
python3 scripts/render-promo.py --ffmpeg /path/to/ffmpeg
```

The renderer reads identity, project IDs, screenshots, and project facts from `src/data/portfolioData.js`. Promotional titles and timing are in the renderer. The website domain is ardhrubo.dev, matching `index.html` and the sitemap; update the renderer, captions, and transcript if that changes.

`#promo` uses native video playback with no autoplay. Video bytes load only when the promo view is opened (metadata preload). Links download the original exports. Review the final MP4 after any content or timing changes.
